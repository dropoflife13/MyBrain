# [ECO] Economy & Macroeconomics — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational economic invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Economics studies the allocation of scarce resources that have alternative uses, governed by incentives, marginal trade-offs, and price discovery.

## Rules (`ECO-01` .. `ECO-99`)

- **ECO-01** (Opportunity Cost): WHEN evaluating any resource allocation, policy, or capital commitment → DO measure the true cost of the decision as the value of the next best alternative forgone, not merely the explicit monetary outlay | why: Friedrich von Wieser and Lionel Robbins established that scarcity forces every choice to sacrifice the highest-valued unchosen alternative | breaks: When resources are genuinely non-rivalrous and non-scarce with zero marginal reproduction cost | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #economy #opportunity-cost ^eco-01
- **ECO-02** (Incentive Compatibility & Goodhart's Law): WHEN designing economic policies, compensation schemes, or market rules → DO align individual incentives with system outcomes and anticipate that when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure | why: Lucas Critique and Goodhart's Law show rational agents optimize against the rule itself, altering the underlying statistical relationship | breaks: Intrinsically motivated small-group cooperation governed by high social trust rather than extrinsic metric targets | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #economy #incentives ^eco-02
- **ECO-03** (Comparative Advantage): WHEN structuring trade or division of labor between entities → DO allocate production based on lowest relative opportunity cost rather than absolute productivity | why: David Ricardo (1817) proved that mutual gains from trade arise whenever relative opportunity costs differ between parties | breaks: Strategic national-security supply chains (e.g., energy, defense, critical semiconductors) where geopolitical resilience overrides pure comparative cost efficiency | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #economy #trade ^eco-03
