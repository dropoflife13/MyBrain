# [NWS] News & Media Epistemology — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational media-literacy and source-verification invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- News media is a lossy, incentive-driven filter over raw events; epistemic rigor requires tracing claims back to primary documents while stripping editorial framing.

## Rules (`NWS-01` .. `NWS-99`)

- **NWS-01** (Primary Artifact Traceability): WHEN evaluating a breaking news report or viral headline → DO trace the claim back to its underlying primary artifact (court filing, peer-reviewed paper, statutory text, or unedited transcript) before treating secondary characterization as fact | why: Secondary reporting introduces compression loss, headline sensationalism, and telephone-game distortion (ARB-03, RS-01) | breaks: Eyewitness breaking events where no written primary document exists yet, in which case claims must be marked provisional pending multi-source corroboration (RS-04) | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #news #verification ^nws-01
- **NWS-02** (Incentive & Framing Audit): WHEN synthesizing media coverage across outlets → DO explicitly separate verifiable factual occurrences (who, what, when, where) from editorial adjectives, selective omission, and agenda-setting framing | why: Lippmann (Public Opinion) and Chomsky/Herman (Manufacturing Consent) demonstrate that institutional funding, audience capture, and access journalism shape story selection and tone | breaks: Verbatim archival logging of an outlet's editorial stance where the framing itself is the subject of study | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #news #framing ^nws-02
- **NWS-03** (Lindy Signal vs. Recency Noise): WHEN weighing the structural importance of a news event → DO discount high-frequency 24-hour outrage cycles in favor of slow-moving structural indicators (legislative enactments, capital flows, demographic shifts, balance-sheet changes) | why: Nassim Taleb's Noise/Signal ratio shows that sampling information at higher frequency increases the proportion of random noise relative to structural signal | breaks: Acute emergency response or real-time operational incident monitoring where immediate minute-by-minute telemetry is required | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #news #signal ^nws-03
