# [EDU] Education & Pedagogy — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational learning-science invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Durable learning is a generative biological process of active retrieval, spaced consolidation, and progressive scaffolding from concrete examples to abstract transfer.

## Rules (`EDU-01` .. `EDU-99`)

- **EDU-01** (Active Retrieval & Spaced Repetition): WHEN designing a learning curriculum or mastery assessment → DO prioritize active recall testing spaced across expanding intervals over passive re-reading or highlighting | why: Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve and Roediger & Karpicke's Testing Effect prove that effortful memory retrieval strengthens synaptic consolidation far more than passive exposure | breaks: Initial first-contact orientation where foundational vocabulary must be decoded before retrieval is possible | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #education #retrieval ^edu-01
- **EDU-02** (Zone of Proximal Development & Scaffolding): WHEN sequencing instructional difficulty → DO calibrate challenges just beyond the learner's unassisted capability (Vygotsky's ZPD) and fade scaffolding as procedural fluency is demonstrated | why: Tasks that are too easy induce boredom, while tasks that exceed working-memory scaffolding induce cognitive overload (Sweller's Cognitive Load Theory) | breaks: Standardized summative certification exams where scaffolding must be removed to measure independent mastery | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #education #scaffolding ^edu-02
