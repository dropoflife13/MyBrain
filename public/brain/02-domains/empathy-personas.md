# User Personas & Empathy Maps

Before any system is built, we define the human. 

## The Empathy Map Framework
- **Says:** What quotes or phrases do they use? (e.g., "I just need this to work fast.")
- **Thinks:** What worries them? (e.g., "Am I going to lose my data if the internet drops?")
- **Does:** What is their current behavior? (e.g., Writes it down on paper first, then types it later.)
- **Feels:** What is their emotional state? (e.g., Frustrated, overwhelmed, afraid of making a mistake.)

## Target Persona Archetypes
- **Primary User:** LGU administrative officer / field inspector. Device: entry-level Android on 3G network. High anxiety around irreversible data loss.
- **Secondary User:** IT systems administrator / support desk. Needs unambiguous attribution, clear audit logs, and one-click data recovery.
