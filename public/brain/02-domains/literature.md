# [LIT] Literature — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational narrative and rhetorical invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Literature encodes human structural conflict, causal agency, and symbolic meaning through disciplined narrative architecture and rhetorical balance.

## Rules (`LIT-01` .. `LIT-99`)

- **LIT-01** (Aristotelian Narrative Arc): WHEN structuring or analyzing a complete narrative → DO verify that it possesses a unified plot with a beginning, middle, and end, driven by causal necessity and character agency (Ethos) rather than episodic coincidence | why: Aristotle's Poetics establishes that dramatic unity and catharsis depend on probable or necessary causal progression ("but/therefore" rather than "and then") | breaks: In postmodern or abstract art, where plot and linear causality are intentionally discarded | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #literature #narrative ^lit-01
- **LIT-02** (Rhetorical Triangle): WHEN evaluating persuasive prose or literary address → DO balance Ethos (speaker credibility and character), Pathos (emotional resonance), and Logos (logical structure and evidence) | why: Aristotle's Rhetoric demonstrates that persuasion fails when any leg of the triad is severed from the others | breaks: Purely formal mathematical proofs or raw telemetry logs where emotional appeal (Pathos) is extraneous | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #literature #rhetoric ^lit-02
- **LIT-03** (Close Reading & Textual Primacy): WHEN interpreting a literary text → DO anchor claims in direct textual evidence (diction, syntax, imagery, and structural motifs) before invoking speculative biographical assumptions | why: Wimsatt and Beardsley's Intentional Fallacy warns against substituting authorial biography for what the text itself enacts | breaks: Historical-archival philology where reconstructing the author's biographical timeline is the explicit research objective | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #literature #criticism ^lit-03
