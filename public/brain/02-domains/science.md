# [SCI] Natural Sciences & Empirical Method — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational scientific method invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Science advances not by proving theories true, but by subjecting bold, quantitative conjectures to rigorous experimental attempts at refutation.

## Rules (`SCI-01` .. `SCI-99`)

- **SCI-01** (Falsifiability): WHEN evaluating whether a hypothesis or model is scientific → DO require that it make risky, specific predictions capable of being empirically proven false by a conceivable observation | why: Karl Popper (The Logic of Scientific Discovery) established falsifiability as the demarcation criterion between empirical science and unfalsifiable pseudoscience | breaks: Purely formal mathematical axioms or definitional tautologies, which are evaluated by deductive consistency rather than empirical falsification | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #science #falsifiability ^sci-01
- **SCI-02** (Independent Replication & Control): WHEN assessing an experimental finding → DO require independent replication with blinded controls and pre-registered protocols before elevating a single study to scientific consensus | why: Ioannidis (2005) demonstrated that underpowered studies, p-hacking, and publication bias render isolated single-study claims highly prone to false positives | breaks: Rare astronomical or geological events (e.g., a specific supernova observation) where controlled laboratory manipulation is replaced by multi-instrument independent observation | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #science #replication ^sci-02
- **SCI-03** (Conservation Laws & Dimensional Consistency): WHEN formulating or auditing a physical model → DO verify that equations are dimensionally homogeneous and obey fundamental conservation laws (energy, momentum, charge, mass-energy) | why: Noether's Theorem links every continuous symmetry of nature to a strict conservation law; any model violating dimensional or conservation invariance is physically invalid | breaks: Cosmological general relativity over expanding FLRW metrics where global time-translation symmetry (and thus simple global energy conservation) requires covariant stress-energy tensor formulation | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #science #physics ^sci-03
