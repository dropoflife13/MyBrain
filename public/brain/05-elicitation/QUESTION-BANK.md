# Question Bank

IDs `Q-nn`, reused across sessions. Add a question when a gap opens.

## Universal
- **Q-01** What is the first thing you notice that others miss?
- **Q-02** What rule do you follow that is not written anywhere?
- **Q-03** What do you believe that most of your peers do not?
- **Q-04** When do you break your own rules?
- **Q-05** What mistake have you made more than once?
- **Q-06** What is your smell test, the thing that makes you slow down?
- **Q-07** What do you refuse to do even when the client asks?
- **Q-08** Which decision did you reverse, and what changed your mind?
- **Q-09** How do you know you are done?
- **Q-10** What is the cheapest test you always run?
- **Q-11** What do you over-engineer on purpose, and why?
- **Q-12** What do you deliberately under-invest in, and why?

## Cybersecurity (CS)
- **Q-20** Where do you look first: identity, boundary, or data? Why?
- **Q-21** Which "compliant but insecure" pattern do you see most often?
- **Q-22** When is "secure enough" enough?
- **Q-23** Which threat do people underrate?
- **Q-24** How do you set severity when CVSS and the business disagree?
- **Q-25** When do you stop testing?

## Solutions (SO)
- **Q-40** What is your default bias: buy, build, or adapt?
- **Q-41** When do you simplify and when do you generalize?
- **Q-42** What is your "this will fail in 18 months" tell?
- **Q-43** How do you scope a first slice?
- **Q-44** What is your first move on a blank page?

## Business Analysis (BA)
- **Q-30** How do you tell a requirement from a solution in disguise?
- **Q-31** What is the first question you ask a stakeholder?
- **Q-32** What are the tells that a project will fail?
- **Q-33** How do you handle a stakeholder who will not commit?
- **Q-34** When do you say no to scope, and how?

## Cross-pollination (type D, T8)
- **Q-50** If your security instinct reviewed this BA deliverable, what would it flag?
- **Q-51** What does your solutions mindset think is wrong with your security review?

## Memory triggers (T9)
- **Q-60** What do you check on every project before anyone asks?
- **Q-61** When did you last say "this will not work" and turn out right? What did you see?
- **Q-62** What do juniors do that makes you wince?
- **Q-63** Which template, tool, or shortcut do you keep reusing, and why that one?
- **Q-64** Which lesson from your worst project do you still apply?
- **Q-65** Which advice from a mentor or teacher do you still follow without thinking?
- **Q-66** Which phrase do you repeat in reviews?
- **Q-67** What goes into the first five minutes of a handover?
- **Q-68** When were you the calmest person in the room? Why?
- **Q-69** What can you predict about projects that others cannot?
- **Q-70** What would you check on day one at a new organization?
- **Q-71** What did you used to believe that you no longer do?

## Teach-back (T7)
- **Q-80** Explain to a sharp junior, in five rules, how you review a system's security.
- **Q-81** Explain, in five rules, how you scope a first slice of a solution.
- **Q-82** Explain, in five rules, how you find out what a stakeholder actually needs.
