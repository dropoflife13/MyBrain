6. **[UI-02] Interaction Discipline & Theme:** Restrained craft: Inline `<head>` script to kill FOUC, semantic tokens only, solid-color darkening on hover, and WCAG AA contrast.
7. **[IR-01] Offline Reconciliation & Clock Skew:** Server-authoritative boundaries: Server stamps `received_at = now()`, bounds client `observed_at` within skew tolerance, uses `UNIQUE` idempotency keys (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), and processes items individually.
8. **[SO-02] Read Scaling Order:** Follow the disciplined scaling order: Indexing (`EXPLAIN ANALYZE`) → Query optimization → CDN/Edge caching → Materialized read models (concurrent refresh) → Read replicas before touching distributed queues or sharding.
