# S-02 — Gap Closure Across All 4 Domains (CS-06, SO-06, BA-06, UI-06)

Targeted boundary & threshold session resolving the 4 open domain gaps queued in `GAP-FINDER.md`.

## Raw Answers & Decisions

1. **[S-02.1 — CS-06: Multi-Tenant Verification Threshold]:** The 5-Role Matrix Test: Before any migration ships, test real read/write queries across all 5 actor contexts (`anon` visitor, `student/user`, `coordinator/officer`, `supervisor/adviser`, `admin`) + verify cross-tenant isolation (`School A` cannot read `School B`). Automated `pgTAP` runs on CI; manual role-matrix verification is the minimum for MVP sprints.
2. **[S-02.2 — SO-06: Sync vs. Async Batch Upload Cutoff]:** The 500-Row / 2-Second Boundary: Synchronous server validation (with row-level error preview before commit) for ≤500 rows (~1 cohort/semester upload). Once batches exceed 500 rows or 2s execution time, chunk on the client or offload to an async outbox job with a real-time progress bar.
3. **[S-02.3 — BA-06: Free Pilot to Paid Conversion Playbook]:** Data-Moat Lock-In + Founding-Partner Tier: During the free 1-cycle pilot, make sure the school/business accumulates irreplaceable audit history (signed MOAs, verified hours, evaluations) and measure exact hours saved. At renewal, offer a Founding-School discount or per-active-student pricing (~₱150/sem) that costs less than the paper/admin labor it replaced, while keeping historical read-export access fair so they never feel held hostage.
4. **[S-02.4 — UI-06: Mobile-First vs. Desktop-First Tie-Breaker]:** Role-Matched Viewport Priority: Design mobile-first (375px, thumb-friendly cards, bottom/drawer nav) for field/on-the-go roles (Students browsing/logging hours, Market Inspectors in MerkadoTrack), and desktop-first (1440px, high-density sortable tables, keyboard actions, contained horizontal scroll on mobile) for batch-review roles (Coordinators, Treasurers, Admins).
