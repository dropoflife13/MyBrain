# [MKT] Marketing & Brand Strategy — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational positioning and behavioral adoption invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Effective marketing is category positioning and trust compounding: aligning a specific audience's acute job-to-be-done with an unmistakable, credible value proposition.

## Rules (`MKT-01` .. `MKT-99`)

- **MKT-01** (Jobs-to-be-Done & Problem Primacy): WHEN crafting product messaging or positioning → DO anchor on the specific progress the customer is struggling to make in a concrete circumstance (the "Job to be Done") rather than listing internal product features | why: Clayton Christensen's Jobs-to-be-Done framework and Theodore Levitt ("people don't want a quarter-inch drill, they want a quarter-inch hole") show buyers hire solutions to resolve functional, emotional, and social friction | breaks: Commodity spec-sheet procurement where buyers filter strictly by standardized technical tolerances (e.g., M8 bolt tensile strength) | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #marketing #jtbd ^mkt-01
- **MKT-02** (Positioning & Category Distinctiveness): WHEN entering a crowded market → DO claim a distinct, defensible attribute or niche segment (Ries & Trout Positioning) rather than claiming to be marginally "better" on the incumbent's dimension | why: Human memory allocates limited mental availability per category; undifferentiated "me-too" messaging reinforces the market leader | breaks: Regulated utility monopolies where category choice does not exist | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #marketing #positioning ^mkt-02
- **MKT-03** (Diffusion of Innovations — Crossing the Chasm): WHEN launching a new product → DO saturate a focused beachhead of pragmatist early adopters with a complete whole-product solution before attempting broad mainstream horizontal expansion | why: Everett Rogers (Diffusion of Innovations) and Geoffrey Moore (Crossing the Chasm) proved that mainstream pragmatists reference peer proof within their own vertical before adopting | breaks: Viral zero-switching-cost consumer entertainment where horizontal network effects spread instantaneously across segments | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #marketing #adoption ^mkt-03
