# [ART] Art & Aesthetics — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational compositional and aesthetic invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Visual art organizes perception through intentional hierarchy, contrast, proportion, and the tension between formal constraint and expressive disruption.

## Rules (`ART-01` .. `ART-99`)

- **ART-01** (Intentional Structural Hierarchy): WHEN composing or critiquing a visual or narrative work → DO establish a coherent structural progression (focal entry, development, and resolution) driven by deliberate visual causality | why: Classical aesthetic theory from Aristotle's Poetics to Renaissance composition requires unified structural order so the viewer's eye traverses a purposeful path | breaks: In postmodern, Dadaist, or abstract expressionist art, where linear structure and representational causality are intentionally discarded | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #art #composition ^art-01
- **ART-02** (Gestalt Perceptual Grouping): WHEN arranging visual elements in space → DO leverage proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, and figure-ground separation to control how the human visual cortex groups forms into a unified whole | why: Max Wertheimer and Gestalt perceptual psychology show the brain perceives structured configurations before individual parts | breaks: Optical illusions or camouflage art designed deliberately to destabilize figure-ground resolution | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #art #gestalt ^art-02
- **ART-03** (Contrast & Negative Space): WHEN establishing focal emphasis in a composition → DO pair high-contrast focal regions with deliberate negative space (rest areas) so visual signal is not drowned out by uniform noise | why: Information theory and Japanese Ma (negative space) aesthetics demonstrate that emphasis only exists relative to surrounding stillness | breaks: Horror vacui (all-over maximalist patterns or Jackson Pollock drip paintings) where uniform surface density is the intended field effect | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #art #aesthetics ^art-03
