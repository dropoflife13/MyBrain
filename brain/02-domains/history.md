# [HIS] History & Historiography — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational historiographical invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- History is not a deterministic script but an empirical reconstruction of past human agency, institutional incentives, and material constraints from primary sources.

## Rules (`HIS-01` .. `HIS-99`)

- **HIS-01** (Primary Source Criticism & Provenance): WHEN reconstructing a historical event → DO weight contemporaneous primary archival documents and material archaeology over secondary retellings, while auditing the primary author's proximity, incentives, and audience | why: Leopold von Ranke's source-critical method (Quellenkritik) establishes that proximity to the event and corroboration across adversarial witnesses are required to filter propaganda | breaks: Pre-literate eras or destroyed archives where oral traditions, comparative linguistics, and physical stratigraphy are the sole surviving evidence | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #history #historiography ^his-01
- **HIS-02** (Guard Against Presentism & Teleology): WHEN evaluating historical actors or institutions → DO judge their decisions against the information, material constraints, and normative frameworks available to them at the time, rather than assuming history inevitably marched toward the present (Whig history) | why: Herbert Butterfield (The Whig Interpretation of History) showed that projecting modern hindsight onto past actors distorts causal understanding | breaks: Explicitly normative legal/ethical retrospectives assessing long-term institutional restitution | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #history #context ^his-02
- **HIS-03** (Structural Longue Durée vs. Contingency): WHEN explaining major historical transitions → DO distinguish slow-moving structural forces (demographics, climate, agrarian/industrial technology — Braudel's longue durée) from contingent triggering events | why: Fernand Braudel and the Annales School demonstrated that surface political events are bounded by deeper material and geographic structures | breaks: Tightly bounded tactical military or diplomatic micro-histories spanning hours or days | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #history #structures ^his-03
