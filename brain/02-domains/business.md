# [BIZ] Business Strategy & Unit Economics — How I Think

> Status: `active` (3 active rules). Foundational unit-economic and competitive strategy invariants seeded under ARB-07 first-principles grounding.

## Mental Model & Worldview

- Sustainable enterprises create differentiated customer value while capturing a durable margin defended by structural moats and positive unit economics.

## Rules (`BIZ-01` .. `BIZ-99`)

- **BIZ-01** (Unit Economics Invariant — LTV > CAC): WHEN scaling customer acquisition or evaluating a business model → DO verify that Customer Lifetime Value (LTV, net of gross margin) exceeds Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by a sustainable multiple (typically >= 3x) with a bounded payback period | why: Scaling negative contribution margin accelerates cash burn and insolvency rather than compounding enterprise value | breaks: Loss-leader hardware or regulated network utilities where verified contractual lock-in guarantees multi-year downstream monetization | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #business #unit-economics ^biz-01
- **BIZ-02** (Structural Competitive Moats): WHEN assessing long-term profit durability → DO identify a structural barrier to entry (network effects, high switching costs, counter-positioning, scale economies, or proprietary regulatory/IP assets) rather than relying on temporary execution speed | why: Michael Porter's Five Forces and Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers demonstrate that unmoated excess returns are competed away to the cost of capital | breaks: Short-lived arbitrage or opportunistic trading windows where long-term defensibility is not the objective | conf: 5 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #business #strategy ^biz-02
- **BIZ-03** (Contribution Margin & Operating Leverage): WHEN analyzing cost structure → DO separate variable COGS (which scale linearly with volume) from fixed operating expenses so contribution margin per unit is never obscured by top-line GMV | why: Confusing gross merchandise volume with net contribution margin masks structural unprofitability | breaks: Pure public-good grants or non-profit philanthropic distributions funded by endowments rather than unit revenue | conf: 4 | ev: [[CONVENTIONS]] [[OPERATING-PRINCIPLES]] [[arbiter]] | status: active #business #margins ^biz-03
