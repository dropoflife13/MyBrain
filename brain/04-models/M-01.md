# M-01 — Understand First

## Essence
Understand the problem and the people it touches before choosing a solution, then still decide.

## When I reach for it
A new problem, a conflict between people or agents, or a decision that is hard to undo.

## How I apply it
1. Frame: one sentence, split into known / assumed / unknown.
2. Hear: for each affected person or role, what they want, fear, could lose; mark unknowns, do not guess.
3. Challenge: the strongest case against my leaning, and what evidence would change my mind.
4. Decide: reversible or one-way; spend more understanding only on one-way calls; end with a decision, an owner, and "now" or "on <date> with <data>".
5. Hand off: which agents, in what order, by rule ID; the decision is recorded in the project's own ADR.

## Where it breaks
Over-understanding delays decisions; seeing every side can soften a hard call; an AI assistant tends to mirror the owner.

## Example from my work
Illustrative, not yet the owner's own: MerkadoTrack panel question on fraudulent inspector prices (A-01). A real price shock looks like an anomaly, so a flag should read 'needs review, reason shown', not 'suspected fraud'. Owner to replace with a real example.
